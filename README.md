# Glyph

![Glyph](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/247f9a94-c827-4b40-94b4-52693fa2979d)

## What is it?

its a techwear keychain, designed with alot of things in mind including the holy drip. pretty much a blinky with a chase pattern

## why

- it is cool
- holy drip
- i never made a blinky

## how does it work

it uses a 555 ic with another ic for the chase pattern, pretty basic on the surface

## motivation

i saw alot of techwear but they are hella expensive so i decided to make my own oss version!
